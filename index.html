      Recipe Scaling Tips

      Use the scaling buttons on any recipe page for quick ½, 1.5×, or double adjustments. For precise quantity scaling of complex recipes, ask your family recipe assistant.

      Common conversions:
      1 cup = 16 tbsp = 48 tsp ≈ 237 ml

      1 stick butter = ½ cup = 8 tbsp

      Family Notes & Traditions

      About Our Cookbook: This digital cookbook preserves the recipes that have brought the Schuitema family together for generations. Many originated in the Netherlands and were adapted over the years.

      How to Contribute: Use the "Add Recipe" section to create a new recipe, review the PDF, then share it with the family recipe assistant to have it permanently added.

      General Tips: Always measure flour by spooning into the cup and leveling. For Dutch recipes, "boerenkool" is curly kale. Many desserts taste even better the next day.

      Add more family stories, substitutions, or seasonal notes here over time by asking your assistant to update this section.

      About This Cookbook

      Welcome to the Schuitema Family Recipes website — a modern way to keep our old-fashioned recipes alive and easily accessible.

      This site is designed to feel like an old cookbook you might find on Oma's shelf: warm parchment tones, elegant typography, and space for the notes and stories that make each recipe special.

      • Easy search by name, ingredient, or tag
      • Family notes on every recipe
      • PDF generation for printing or saving
      • Kitchen calculators for scaling and converting
      • Add to your phone's home screen for quick access (PWA)
      • Share the link freely with family

      To add or edit recipes: Talk to your Schuitema Recipe Assistant. It can help create, review, update, or remove recipes and keep this site current.
